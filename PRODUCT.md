# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People discovering Romy Moav through writing, a referral, search, or a social profile. They want to understand the author’s point of view, experience, and areas of curiosity, then find the right place to follow or contact them.

## Product Purpose

The site is a focused personal-brand landing page that turns an introduction into a confident next step: reading the Substack, following a professional profile, or getting in touch.

## Positioning

It presents Romy as a writer and cybersecurity practitioner connecting innovation and business theory with practical knowledge from more than a decade spent in the early incubation phase of startups (0 → 1). The site should feel authored and specific rather than like a generic portfolio template.

## Operating Context

Visitors will scan on phones and laptops, often with limited attention and without prior context. The page must work as a self-contained introduction and remain easy to replace with real biography, project links, and contact details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The initial surface is a static homepage with responsive navigation, a scroll-scrubbed introduction, a short context section, Substack links, social links, and a contact action. No portfolio, backend, CMS, authentication, analytics, or factual performance claims are established.

## Evidence on Hand

No production biography, verified social URLs, testimonials, logos, or project assets are present in the repository. Placeholder URLs must be clearly easy to replace and must not imply real clients or outcomes.

## Product Principles

- Make the point of view legible before the visitor explores.
- Use restraint to signal care, not scarcity.
- Show enough process to make the work feel real without fabricating proof.
- Make contact feel like a natural continuation of the page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts that remain usable without hover.
